## From the iPhone

- **Send** tab: Photos or Files pickers (multi-select) → Mac **Inbox**
- Share sheet: **Send to Mac** from other apps
- If Tether is not connected yet, open it and connect to finish

## On the Mac

- **Inbox** page lists received files with Open, Reveal, Move, Delete
- **Shelf** shows the latest batch for drag-out
- Changing the Inbox folder and Shelf auto-show lives in Settings → General

## Agents

From a live agent, the paperclip attaches a photo or file into Inbox and pastes the Mac path into the agent (without opening the Shelf).
