## fetch

A short Mac portrait: model, OS, CPU, cores, memory, uptime, load, disk, and shares. `neofetch` and `fastfetch` are aliases. This is a Tether portrait, not the real neofetch binary.

## htop

`htop`, `btop`, or `monitor` opens a live view (about every 1.5s) with load, tasks, battery (when present), thermal state, CPU/memory/disk/network, and busiest processes.

There is no kill or renice. Process listing needs the host’s process-info entitlement — quit and relaunch Host after a rebuild if the list is empty.

`htop` here is a Tether screen, not the system `htop` / `btop` program.
