## Guest Wi‑Fi blocks discovery

Many guest networks isolate clients. Use a normal LAN, or join both devices to a phone hotspot.

## Do transfers work in the background?

Keep Tether in the foreground. iOS can suspend the app; a suspended app does not continue the copy. Live Activities may show last-known state — they do not keep chunk transfers running forever.

## Can I browse the whole disk?

No. Only folders you share. `/` on the phone is the share list.

## Is there a general shell?

No. Everyday commands are virtual inside shares. Only allowlisted agent names get a live PTY.

## Windows or Android?

Out of scope for now. Mac + iPhone only.

## How do I reach home from cellular?

Pair on Wi‑Fi first, then set up Tailscale (recommended), a reachable address, or a relay. See [Other networks](/docs/remote).
